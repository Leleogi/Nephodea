About Nephodea

Nephodea is a test version of a cloud type classifier, built as a learning project.

A small image model looks at your whole photo and estimates how likely each of 12 classes is: 11 cloud types, or no clouds. It is right about 3 times out of 4, so treat its answer as a good guess.

Your photo is only used to make the prediction. It is never saved.

The model was trained during a 2024 hackathon. Thanks to the team who helped train it.
