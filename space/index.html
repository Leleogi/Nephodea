        About Nephodea

        Nephodea is a test version of a cloud type classifier, built as a learning project.

        A small image model looks at your whole photo and estimates how likely each of 12 classes is: 11 cloud types, or no clouds. It is right about 3 times out of 4, so treat its answer as a good guess.

        To look at clouds one by one, a second model first finds the sky in your photo. Inside the sky, a simple colour rule tells clouds from blue sky: clear sky is much more blue than red, while clouds are white or grey. The cutoff between the two is chosen from each photo's own colours, and grey pixels count as cloud too, so shaded cloud bases are found. The photo is then split into 9 overlapping parts, each part with enough cloud is classified on its own, and the parts are grouped by the cloud type they show. The colour rule can mistake a hazy or sunset sky for cloud.

        The model runs inside your browser. Your photo is not uploaded anywhere.

        The model was trained during a 2024 hackathon. Thanks to the team who helped train it.

        The sky model is a small U-2-Net by xiongzhu666, and the colour rule comes from Yuhao Nie. Both are shared under the MIT licence.
